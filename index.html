How to calculate food cost

Add each ingredient and enter its price. The calculator adds all ingredient prices, divides the total by the number of portions, and calculates food cost percentage when you enter a selling price.

Food Cost % = Cost Per Portion ÷ Selling Price × 100

Who can use it?

Restaurants, chefs, home food businesses, caterers, cafés and small food businesses can use ChefTools to estimate recipe and portion costs.

FAQ

Can I use PKR and INR?
Yes. Select SAR, PKR, INR, USD, AED, GBP or EUR.

Does it convert currencies?
No. It calculates using the currency you select and the prices you enter.
